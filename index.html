Yasur Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Yasur is a dating app designed to help adults discover connections and build relationships. This Privacy Policy explains how Yasur collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Yasur, information may include:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication details.
  • Profile information you provide, such as photos, gender, pronouns, interests, and relationship preferences.
  • Content you submit or exchange, including messages and other interactions.
  • Photos submitted for profile review or moderation.
  • App usage, diagnostic, and technical information processed by the services that support Yasur.
  • Information related to Sparks, Yasur's in-app virtual currency, and associated transactions or rewards, where applicable.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and maintain Yasur, create and display profiles, support discovery and connections, enable messaging, protect users, moderate submitted photos, troubleshoot problems, and support in-app features and transactions.

3. Photos and Content Moderation

Photos may be processed using automated moderation services to help identify content that violates app rules. Information submitted for moderation may be processed by third-party service providers to perform this function.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

Yasur relies on third-party services to support app functionality, which may include Firebase and Google Cloud services. These providers may process information on Yasur's behalf. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

5. In-App Currency and Advertising

Yasur uses a virtual currency called Sparks for certain in-app features. Depending on available features, users may be able to obtain Sparks through in-app purchases or other app activities. Payment processing and advertising services may process relevant information under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Yasur, provide its features, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies. We use reasonable measures designed to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You should provide accurate profile information and avoid sharing information you do not want other users to see. You may request account or personal-data deletion by contacting Yasur using the contact information provided below. Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law.

8. Adults Only

Yasur is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly permit children under 18 to use the service. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Yasur's features or practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Yasur team at: yasuradmin@gmail.com
