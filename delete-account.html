Yasur Account Deletion

Yasur lets users request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Yasur and sign in.
  2. Go to your account or profile settings.
  3. Select the Delete Account option and follow the confirmation steps.

What gets deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, Yasur will delete the account and associated personal data that is eligible for deletion, including profile information and photos, subject to applicable legal, security, and fraud-prevention requirements.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account, email yasuradmin@gmail.com with the subject “Yasur Account Deletion Request.” Do not include your password.

Processing and retention

Requests are reviewed and processed as soon as reasonably possible. Any data that must be retained for legal or security reasons will be retained only as permitted or required.
